“Wall Art” is a piece that I created in L5 Lua. I wanted to make an interactive piece that allowed the viewer to rearrange the art on the walls randomly, change the color of the background randomly, and randomly generate an inspiring artistic quote. I created all of the art pieces in Adobe Photoshop. I used a riso color palette to ensure the colors would print well on the riso graph. I wanted to create my own art because I felt that it added a more creative touch, and I love painting outside of my classes, and I thought it would be really fun to create my own art to emphasize this. I chose to make an abstract piece because I felt it went better with the aesthetic. 
With regard to patterns and randomness, I used randomization to randomly reorder the paintings I created inside the different frames, to randomly generate a different background color, and to randomly pull an inspiring artistic quote from the list that I inserted into my main Lua file. I was originally inspired by tile patterns and by a pattern I saw that incorporated many different squares. After pondering for a while about what I wanted this project to be about, I noticed that the square pattern I was admiring was reminiscent of a gallery wall. When creating my different paintings, I thought a lot about pattern and randomness. In some of the pieces, I emphasized more pattern, and in others I emphasized randomness. This was another reason that I really pushed towards abstraction in the paintings that I created in Photoshop because I felt that it sent a clearer message about randomness and pattern.
