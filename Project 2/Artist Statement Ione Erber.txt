“Wall Art” is a piece that I created in L5 Lua. I wanted to make an interactive piece that allowed the viewer to rearrange the art on the walls randomly , change the color of the background randomly, and randomly generate an inspiring artistic quote. I created all of the art pieces in Adobe Photoshop. I used a riso color palate to get colors that would be processed well by the riso graph. I wanted to create my own art because I felt that it added a more creative touch and I love painting outside of my classes and I thought it would be really fun to create my own art to emphasize this. I chose to make an abstract piece because I felt it went better with the aesthetic. 
With regards to patterns and randomness, I used randomization to randomly reorder the paintings I created inside the different frames, to randomly generate a different background color, and to randomly pull an inspiring artistic quote from the list that I inserted into my main lua file. I was originally inspired by the patterns of tiles and of this pattern that I saw that incorporated a lot of different squares. After pondering for a while about what I wanted this project to be about, I noticed that the square pattern I was admiring was reminiscent of a gallery wall. When creating my different paintings, I thought a lot about pattern and randomness. In some of the pieces, I emphasized more pattern and in others I emphasized randomness. This was another reason that I really pushed towards abstraction in the paintings that I created in Photoshop because I felt that it sent a more clear message about randomness and pattern.
